Order food from nearby restaurants with RedRush. Browse menus, place cash-on-delivery orders, track deliveries live, and receive real-time order updates.

For customers
• Discover nearby restaurants using accurate shop locations.
• Browse menus and add meals to your cart.
• Place secure cash-on-delivery orders.
• Follow every order from confirmation to delivery.
• View the rider's progress and delivery proximity on the live map.
• Contact support when you need help.

For restaurants
• Receive and manage incoming customer orders.
• Update preparation and pickup status.
• Maintain restaurant details, menu items, opening status, and location.

For delivery riders
• Find available delivery requests nearby.
• Follow the required pickup and delivery workflow.
• Share current location during active delivery tracking.

RedRush brings customers, restaurants, and riders together in one simple food-delivery experience.
